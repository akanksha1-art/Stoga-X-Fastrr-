Part One — Checkout

What's actually different, in six points

Not a prettier payment screen. A checkout built on the same network that's already carrying Stoga's parcels — Stoga's Track Order page already points to Shiprocket — so what it knows at checkout is what actually happens after.

01

One network, from click to doorstep

Address data, delivery dates, and return risk all come from real delivered shipments — not a payments-only dataset guessing at what happens after dispatch.

8L+ deliveries every day
02

Addresses that arrive pre-verified

A returning customer's address is recognised and pre-filled from Shiprocket's own delivered-shipment history, so fewer typos become failed deliveries.

95%+ pre-fill · up to 99% Tier 1
03

Delivery dates you can promise

A pincode-accurate EDD on the product page, from live data across 42 couriers. For a considered purchase like adjustable dumbbells, a real date shortens the decision.

96%+ accuracy · 12–22% PDP-to-cart lift
04

Return risk scored before it ships

COD orders are tagged for risk — high-refusal pincodes, first-time buyers — before dispatch. On heavy gear, every RTO costs more, so catching it early matters more.

Up to 45% RTO reduction
05

Payments that stay out of the way

PayU-powered checkout with smart routing that reroutes on failure, and Quick Pay that pins each customer's preferred UPI app as one tap.

UPI at 0.9%
06

Trust and affordability on bigger baskets

Pay After Delivery for the first-time buyer who wants proof before paying, and Snapmint instalments for the higher-ticket kits and dumbbells.

PAD + Snapmint, built in

Why It Fits Stoga

Three things about this catalogue that make the case

Heavy gear makes returns expensive

Dumbbells, kits, and mats are bulky parcels. A returned order costs freight both ways — so risk tagging and address accuracy pay back faster here than on a light-parcel brand.

An accessory-heavy basket

Belts, wraps, gloves, bands, shakers — most of Stoga's range is a natural add-on. At a ₹1,000 AOV, a smarter cart is the cheapest way to lift the basket.

Trust is already earned

A 4.98 average across 225 reviews is hard-won. Checkout shouldn't be the step that quietly gives it back.

Every number here comes from the same network

fastrr sits on Shiprocket's own delivery infrastructure, so EDD, address pre-fill, and risk scoring are drawn from real completed shipments — not projections.

Part Two — Engage

The order is where the customer's training starts, not where the relationship ends

Stoga has already built something most gear brands haven't — dozens of genuinely useful workout guides, from a 20-minute beginner kettlebell routine to a 12-week push-up plan. Engage turns that library into a retention engine, on the channels customers actually read.

WhatsApp — marketing, utility & chat

Drops, restocks, and cart recovery on marketing; confirmations and shipping updates on utility; and customer-initiated sessions that cost nothing per message.

Instagram & WhatsApp chatbots

Both included in the platform fee — answering product, sizing, and order questions in the DMs where a fitness audience already spends its time.

AI Calling for COD confirmation

An AI call to verify a COD order before it ships — a direct line to fewer failed deliveries on heavy parcels.

Email, SMS & RCS

The long-form channel for workout programmes, the fast channel for alerts, and RCS for richer, tappable messages where supported.

AI Assist Chatbot on the site

An on-site shopping assistant for the "which dumbbells, which belt size" questions — answering instantly, with an add-to-cart card built into the chat.

A dedicated Growth Manager

Included in the platform fee — a named person running journeys, testing, and reporting, so Engage doesn't become another dashboard nobody opens.
